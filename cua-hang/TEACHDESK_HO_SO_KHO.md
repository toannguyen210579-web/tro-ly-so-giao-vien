### Mô tả đầy đủ (dưới 4000)
TeachDesk puts the small jobs of a teaching day in one app, so you spend less time on admin and more time teaching. It works in 21 languages and keeps your class list on your own device.

IN THE CLASSROOM
• Timer: a large countdown you can show on a TV or projector.
• Name picker: choose a student at random, with no repeats until everyone has had a turn.
• Groups: split the class into random groups in one tap and copy the list.
• Scoreboard: keep team scores during games and quizzes.
• Attendance: mark present, late or absent and copy the summary.
• Crossword: build a keyword crossword for revision and reveal it row by row on the big screen.

CHECKING UNDERSTANDING
• Answer cards: print one card per student. Students turn the card to show A, B, C or D, you take a photo of the class, and the app reads every answer. No student devices needed.
• Grade quizzes: enter the answer key once, then tap in each student's answers. Scores and class statistics are calculated for you, and you can export the results.

PAPERWORK
• Scan to PDF: photograph pages, straighten them automatically and save them as one PDF. You can combine scans, photos and existing PDF files.
• ID photo: remove the background, choose blue or white, and get a print sheet in common sizes (35 x 45 mm, 2 x 2 in, 4 x 6 cm).

AI ASSISTANT
Six helpers that write a first draft for you to check and adapt:
• Quiz maker with answer key
• Lesson plan
• Rewrite a text for a reading level
• Worksheet with answers
• Marking rubric
• Feedback on student writing
The AI answers in the language you choose in the app. A limited number of AI requests is free each day. AI can make mistakes, so always check the result before you use it.

PRIVACY
• No account and no sign-up.
• Your class list, scores, quizzes and files stay on your device.
• The classroom tools work without an internet connection.
• Only the AI assistant sends what you type to our server so that an AI service can write the answer. We do not sell data and we do not show ads.

LANGUAGES
English, Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Czech, Romanian, Hungarian, Turkish, Indonesian, Malay, Thai, Khmer, Lao, Burmese and Japanese.

## C. Ghi chú cho người duyệt (App Store, tiếng Anh)
TeachDesk is a set of utilities for school teachers. No account or sign-in is needed; open the app and every tool is available from the home screen.
- Class list: tap "Class list" on the home screen and type a few names, one per line. Name picker, Groups, Attendance and Answer cards use this list.
- Answer cards: tap "Print cards" to create a PDF of printable cards. To test reading without printing, open the PDF on another screen and tap "Scan the class".
- Camera and photo access are used only for Scan to PDF, ID photo and Answer cards. Images are processed on the device and are not uploaded.
- AI assistant: the text typed by the teacher is sent to our server (Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API to write the answer. There is a free daily limit per device. No account is created and the text is not stored by us after the answer is returned.
- The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics.

## F. Nội dung trang chính sách quyền riêng tư (tiếng Anh)
TeachDesk Privacy Policy
Last updated: 28 November 2026

TeachDesk is an app for teachers. We built it so that as little data as possible leaves your device.

What stays on your device
Your class list, attendance, team scores, quizzes and answer keys, crosswords, scanned PDFs, ID photos and answer-card results are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Camera and photos
The camera and the photos you choose are used for Scan to PDF, ID photo and Answer cards. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded.

AI assistant
When you use the AI assistant, the text you type and a random device code created by the app are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes the text to an AI provider (Anthropic) to write the answer. The device code is used only to count your free daily requests. We keep the counter for up to three days. We do not keep the text you typed after the answer is returned. Please do not type students' full names or other personal details into the AI assistant.

What we do not do
We do not ask you to create an account. We do not show ads. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking. We do not sell or rent data.

Children
TeachDesk is for teachers and other adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

## G. Nội dung trang hỗ trợ (tiếng Anh)
TeachDesk Support

Getting started
Open the app and tap "Class list" to type or paste your students' names, one per line. The name picker, groups, attendance and answer cards all use this list.

Common questions
- How do I change the language? Tap the globe button at the top of the home screen.
- Where are my files? PDFs and exported tables are kept inside the app. Use "Share" or "Save to device" to put a copy somewhere else.
- The answer cards are not read. Move closer, use good light, and ask students to hold the cards flat and facing the camera. You can take more than one photo; results add up.
- The AI assistant says I have no requests left. The free requests reset every day.
- Is my class list uploaded? No. It stays on your device.
